About

A designer who thinks beyond aesthetics.

I’m a Senior Product Designer with 13+ years of design experience and 15+ years in IT. I started as a front-end developer, which still shapes how I design: I think about how things are built, not just how they look.

My work spans product design, user experience, mobile apps, web platforms, interaction and motion design. Most recently I led end-to-end UX/UI for Travel Sailo, a premium ride-hailing platform in Dubai — from the first search to checkout, BNPL payments, wallet and loyalty. Before that, as Lead Product Designer at Jamoons Digital, I ran research with more than 100 users on the JLL real estate platform and redesigned how people search for and discover property.

Good design isn’t about making screens look nice. It’s about understanding people, finding the real problem and building something that works for them and for the business. I combine research, strategy, interaction design and careful visual craft, then use data — usability tests, analytics and A/B experiments — to check whether it worked.

I work closely with developers, product managers, marketers and stakeholders, and I enjoy mentoring designers. Twelve years at Rage Communications taught me to lead teams, deliver for clients such as Citibank, and ship on time.

Work/FireBoost

Fitness & Wellness

Making fitness data feel motivating, not overwhelming.

FireBoost is a fitness tracking app with personalised workout recommendations, detailed analytics and activity tracking. The design challenge was presenting dense data — steps, distance, calories, goals — in a way that motivates rather than overwhelms.
